# समुद्र मंथन — Comic Storyboard

This comic uses `content.json` as its canonical content contract. Hindi text
is never generated inside artwork; captions are applied by the browser-based
PDF renderer using the tracked Noto Sans Devanagari font.

## Narrative scope

The nine-panel story follows the Bhagavata Purana Canto 8 sequence:

1. Vishnu advises cooperation between devas and asuras.
2. Mandara and Vasuki become the churning apparatus.
3. Vishnu supports Mandara in the Kurma form.
4. The churning begins.
5. Shiva contains Halahala and becomes Nilakantha.
6. Selected divine treasures emerge.
7. Lakshmi appears and chooses Vishnu.
8. Dhanvantari appears with the amrita vessel.
9. Vishnu assumes the Mohini form and gives amrita to the devas.

## Accuracy guardrails

- Present devas and asuras as disciplined opposing groups, not racialized
  monsters versus humans.
- Vasuki is a dignified naga king and cooperative participant.
- Kurma supports Mandara; the mountain must not float unsupported.
- Shiva contains the poison to protect creation. Keep the event symbolic,
  non-graphic, and suitable for children.
- Only the selected treasures named in the caption need appear. Do not claim
  that one universal ordering of all fourteen treasures is agreed by every
  textual tradition.
- Mohini is Vishnu's divine form. Keep the depiction poised and devotional,
  not sensualized.
- Do not depict Rahu unless the story is explicitly expanded beyond this
  nine-panel scope.

## Reusable lock policy

All visible recurring characters resolve through
`references/character_locks/manifest.json`. Mandara, the Ocean of Milk, and
the treasure procession are comic-specific environment/object designs rather
than recurring character identities.
