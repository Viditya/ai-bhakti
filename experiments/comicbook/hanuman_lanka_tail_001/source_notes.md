# Source notes

Primary narrative source:

- Valmiki Ramayana, Sundara Kanda, Sargas 52-55.
- Sarga 52: Vibhishana argues that an envoy must not be killed.
- Sarga 53: the tail is wrapped and ignited; Sita prays; Agni feels cool;
  Hanuman changes size and escapes.
- Sarga 54: Hanuman crosses Lanka's rooftops and spares Vibhishana's house.
- Sarga 55: he extinguishes the flame, reproaches uncontrolled anger, hears
  that Sita is safe, sees her again, and prepares to depart.
