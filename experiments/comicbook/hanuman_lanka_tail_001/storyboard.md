# हनुमान जी की जलती पूँछ - Comic Storyboard

`content.json` is the canonical content contract. Hindi captions are rendered
outside the artwork with the tracked Noto Sans Devanagari font.

## Narrative scope

The eight-panel story follows Valmiki Ramayana, Sundara Kanda, Sargas 52-55:

1. Hanuman counsels Ravana to return Sita.
2. Vibhishana explains that an envoy must not be killed.
3. Hanuman's tail is wrapped and ignited while Sita prays to Agni.
4. The cool flame does not harm Hanuman, who escapes his bonds.
5. Hanuman leaps across the mansions of Lanka with his blazing tail.
6. He spares Vibhishana's home and extinguishes the flame in the sea.
7. He worries about Sita and reflects on restraining anger.
8. Heavenly witnesses confirm Sita's safety; Hanuman sees her again and leaves.

## Accuracy and child-safety guardrails

- Hanuman is a mature envoy and strategist, not the child stage used in
  `video_001`.
- Ravana, Vibhishana, and Lanka's citizens are dignified people, never
  racialized monsters.
- Fire remains dramatic but non-graphic. Do not show injury, bodies, or
  civilians in danger.
- Sita's prayer and agency are explicit; she is not a passive background prop.
- Preserve the source's moral reflection: courage needs judgment and
  self-control.
- Vibhishana's house is spared.
- Do not add later folklore, modern flags, firearms, or contemporary city
  imagery.
