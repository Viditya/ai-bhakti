# Generation notes

## Shared visual style

- Vertical 2:3 Indian mythological children's comic panels.
- Cinematic painterly rendering with clean linework.
- Night-time Lanka palette: antique gold architecture, indigo sky, vermilion
  fire, and moonlit teal sea.
- No generated text, captions, speech balloons, borders, or watermarks.
- Family-friendly action with no visible injury.

## Character locks

- Hanuman: `hanuman/mature_adult`
- Ravana: `ravana/ageless_mature`
- Vibhishana: `vibhishana/ageless_mature`
- Sita: `sita/young_adult`

Every visible recurring character must use the exact manifest lock. Hanuman's
tail must remain singular, continuous, and visually readable in every panel.

## Environment constants

- Lanka is a magnificent fortified island city on Trikuta, not a generic
  medieval European city.
- Ravana's court uses dark stone, antique gold, crimson textiles, and carved
  rakshasa-lion motifs.
- Ashoka Vatika is moonlit, green, quiet, and spatially separate from the
  burning royal mansions.
