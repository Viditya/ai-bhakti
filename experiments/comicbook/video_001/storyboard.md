# बाल हनुमान और सूर्य - Comic Storyboard

This is an isolated creative experiment based on
`outputs/video_001/script_v2.json`. It does not update `TASK.md`,

## Panel 1 - The mystery

**Caption:** देवराज इंद्र ने बाल हनुमान पर वज्र क्यों चलाया?

Indra's glowing vajra appears high in the celestial sky. Child Hanuman is
silhouetted safely below it against the radiant Sun. The intervention feels
grave and reluctant, never cruel.

## Panel 2 - The rising Sun

**Caption:** एक प्रभात, भूखे बाल हनुमान ने उगते सूर्य को आकाश में लटका हुआ लाल फल समझ लिया।

Child Hanuman stands in a forested mountain setting and looks upward with
innocent delight. The red-orange Sun resembles ripe fruit only by visual
suggestion.

## Panel 3 - The leap

**Caption:** फल पाने की बाल-सुलभ इच्छा में वे एक ही छलांग में आकाश की ओर उड़ चले।

Low-angle action composition. Hanuman launches from a rocky ledge, tail
forming a graceful curve as clouds part along his path.

## Panel 4 - Approaching Surya

**Caption:** वे तेज और ताप से निर्भय होकर सूर्यदेव के निकट पहुँचने लगे।

Hanuman approaches Surya's radiant chariot. Surya is composed and
compassionate. Hanuman does not swallow or hold the Sun.

## Panel 5 - Alarm in heaven

**Caption:** असाधारण घटना देखकर देवलोक में चिंता फैल गई; व्यवस्था की रक्षा का दायित्व इंद्र पर आया।

Indra stands in the foreground with his vajra lowered but ready. Concerned
devas gather behind him while Hanuman and the Sun remain visible far away.

## Panel 6 - The vajra

**Caption:** इंद्र ने वज्र चलाया। प्रहार हनुमान की ठोड़ी पर लगा और वे पर्वत पर मूर्च्छित होकर गिर पड़े।

Show a respectful, non-graphic flash near Hanuman's jaw and his descent
toward a mountain. Indra looks solemn, not triumphant. No blood or cruelty.

## Panel 7 - Boons and destiny

**Caption:** पवनदेव के दुःख से सृष्टि व्याकुल हुई। तब देवताओं ने बाल हनुमान को वरदान दिए - यही शक्तियाँ आगे धर्म की सेवा में समर्पित हुईं।

Vayu cradles Hanuman while the devas offer blessings. Close with a small
visual echo of adult Hanuman leaping toward Lanka.

## Identity lock

- Child proportions, round youthful face, compact athletic body.
- Warm golden-tawny fur, consistent muzzle, ears, hairline, and large eyes.
- Saffron-red child dhoti with the same gold border in every scene.
- Fixed sacred thread, small armlets and anklets.
- Consistent tail thickness, length, and curved tip.
- Small red U-shaped tilak with a central gold line.
- Bareheaded; no adult royal crown.
- Divine, curious, compassionate expression; never monstrous or hyper-muscular.

## Accuracy guardrails

- Use "wanted to reach the Sun as a fruit," not a settled claim that he
  swallowed it.
- Do not present the Sun stopping or all three worlds shaking as precise
  canonical events.
- Restore Vayu's grief and the gods' boons to complete the episode.
- The vajra incident revealed Hanuman's nature and led to boons; it did not
  itself create all his power.
